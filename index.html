How It Works

The Game

My son's football team are running a Premier League Last Man Standing to raise funds.

LMS is simple – each gameweek in the Premier League you need to choose a team.

If you choose a HOME team: They need to WIN to get you through to the next round. If they DRAW or LOSE, you are unfortunately out.

If you choose an AWAY team: They need to WIN or DRAW for you to get through to the next round. If they LOSE, you are out.

Team selection rule: You can only pick a team once. If you choose Arsenal in GW1, then you can't use them again until you have used every team at least once.

The rounds continue until there is just one person left standing.

Special Rules

Splitting the pot: If there are four or fewer people left in the game, they have the opportunity to split the pot. However, every person must agree via a secret ballot. If it's not unanimous, then the gameweek is played.

Everyone knocked out: If everyone is knocked out before there's a winner, the prize pot is rolled over and a new gameweek starts where all players (old and new) can reinvest, giving us a bumper pot.
