My son's football team are running a Premier League Last Man Standing to raise funds.

LMS is simple – each gameweek in the Premier League you need to choose a team.

If that team is a HOME team and they WIN you are through to the next round. If they DRAW or LOSE, you are unfortunately out.

If you choose an AWAY team, they have to WIN or DRAW for you to get through to the next round. If they LOSE, you are out.

The rounds continue until there is just one person left standing – but you can only choose a team ONCE until you have chosen all 20 teams.

If it comes down to the wire

If there are three or fewer people left in the game, they have the opportunity to split the pot, but every person must agree via a secret ballot. If it's not unanimous, then the gameweek is played. And if no one goes through…

If everyone is knocked out before there's a winner, the prize pot is rolled over and a new gameweek starts where all players (old and new) can reinvest in the next game, giving us a bumper pot.

Deadlines

All picks must be submitted at least 90 minutes before the first match. If you don't submit your pick, then you will be eliminated as there's no way to manually insert a selection.

Changed your mind?

If you've changed your mind and want to change your selection, simply submit your new pick using the same name and email address and it'll overwrite your previous pick — as long as it's before the deadline.

Sharing an email address?

If more than one of you is entering from the same email (e.g. a couple both playing), that's fine — just make sure you type your name exactly the same way every time you submit a pick or check your status, so the site can tell you apart.

The pot

The winner gets 50% of the takings, with the remaining 50% going to Horsforth St Margarets.
